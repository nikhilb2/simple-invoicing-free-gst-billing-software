# Printing on A5 and thermal printers

Your PDFs can print on four paper sizes:

- **A4**
- **A5**
- **Thermal 80mm**
- **Thermal 58mm**

## Choose a default

Go to [Settings → Company](../getting-started/company-setup) and pick **Default paper size**. It sits next to the option to show the SKU on the PDF. Click **Save company details**.

The default is used for:

- PDFs you download
- PDFs you email
- Share links you send to customers

## Choose paper for one print

Every document preview has a paper picker next to **Copies**. Use it when one print needs a different size. It changes only that print. Your default stays as it was.

## Which documents can print on thermal

Thermal is offered for:

- Sales invoices and purchase bills
- Payment receipts and vouchers
- Credit notes and debit notes
- Delivery challans
- Quotations and proforma invoices

Reports come in **A4** and **A5** only. These are the ledger statement, Day Book, Tax Ledger, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B. If your default is thermal, reports print on A4.

## 80mm or 58mm

| Size | Printer | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Thermal 80mm** | The counter billing printer | A shop counter |
| **Thermal 58mm** | The small handheld or Bluetooth printer | Billing on the move |

Pick the size that matches the roll in your printer.

## What a thermal bill looks like

The PDF is exactly as long as the bill. There is no blank paper at the end.

If you print copies, each copy (Original, Duplicate) is its own cut. The printer cuts after each one.

Each item shows its HSN, GST rate, quantity × price and amount. Below the items you get:

1. A GST summary by rate
2. Totals
3. The amount in words
4. Bank details
5. The pay QR, if you turned it on

## A5 paper

On A5, the **Total tax** column is left out of the item table so the amounts fit. CGST and SGST, or IGST, still show.

## Printing tips

When the print dialog opens, from your browser or your PDF viewer:

- Choose the paper size that matches your setting.
- Set the scale to **Actual size** or **100%**. Do not use **Fit to page**. It shrinks or stretches the bill.

For a thermal printer, also do this in the printer driver:

- Pick the roll width, 80mm or 58mm.
- Turn off headers and footers.
- Set margins to none.

If the bill prints too small, too large or cut off, check the scale and the roll width first.
